Criteria for the choice of location:

* Facility not too far downstream of a monitoring station measuring both discharge and temperature
* River relatively straight over the study area, no branching, no significant stream flowing into the river
* Pollutant easily characterizable => total chloride / total fluoride / total phosphorus
* Facility with data from latest available year (2024)
* Only point source, water as the medium
